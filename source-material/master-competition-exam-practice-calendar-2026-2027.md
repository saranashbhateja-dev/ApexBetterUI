## Scope and reliability

This calendar has been checked against official organiser pages available on 25 October 2026. “Verified” means the date/window is published by the cited official source; it does not guarantee eligibility, a seat at a particular test centre, or that all personal registration steps are complete. Where a date is still only expected or centre-specific, that limitation is stated explicitly. Dates in India should be interpreted in the relevant organiser’s time zone when one is specified.
